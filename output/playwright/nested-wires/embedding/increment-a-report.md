## What the admitted graph contains

- Construction span keys originate at owner/axis/frame, row, column or child-boundary ordinals, retaining every merged origin. Public coordinate-bearing road IDs are opaque references; none are parsed to recover topology. Driveway provenance includes semantic owner, axis, node, port, side and role.
- The authoritative reservation placement, routing, lane assignment and demand-capacity functions are replayed once for this query. The request's physical roads and allocated lanes must match that retained reservation. Widths use the existing `nestedLaneWidth`: **12 + 12\*d**, including actual **252 / 336 / 480** authoring populations.
- Fixed node dimensions are offsets from single x/y center anchors. Terminal driveway centers equate to their body axes; gate driveway centers equate to their gate anchors. Shared construction spans and registered perpendicular end contacts collapse by provenance. No unlinked street endpoint remains on authoring.
- Root and child bands, ancestor containment, local grid body order, full street envelopes and perpendicular cap extents are represented. Each owned street contributes four envelope containment relations. Every terminal side contributes its body/fan separation; no all-pairs body/road collision discovery is used.
- Each retained travel has exactly one longitudinal relation; every adjacent turn/bridge is recorded as a footprint using the selected projector algebra. Internal turn forward relations, median-column separation, fan depth, and positive stems are included. Nominal unsupported authoring adjustments remain expansion requirements. Existing supported shifts use their actual previous-stem dependency and require exact ordinary segment/owner containment evidence.
- Gates carry full-bundle tangential intervals, including retained turn footprints, both perpendicular contact spans, wall bounds and fixed same-side mouth order. Normal constraints on both sides of the exact wall are separate from tangential fit; a nonempty interval does not erase a normal deficit.
- The graph rejects uncomputable/cross-axis relations, inconsistent contact equalities, positive self-cycles and residual cycles as structured typed failures. Edge directions come from retained construction side/order and selected travel/template directions. Kahn admission uses construction insertion order only to order independent ready vertices; it does not change edges or retry another topology.
