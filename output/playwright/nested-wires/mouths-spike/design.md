### Invariants that must be re-derived, not waived

* **Boundary-safe turn interval:** for this right-to-up gate turn, with street center c, radius R, gate plane g and pitch p, require `c−R+p/2 > g` for a positive owned approach beyond the plane. Equality may create a tangency/contact ambiguity; do not rely on it without proving owned crossing semantics. Current values give 559<576. With all other quantities frozen, the arithmetic deficit is 17 units; that is a diagnostic, **not** a safe global “add 17px” prescription, since moving geometry can change route populations. Mirror the condition for other gate sides and include opposing-left-turn rank extents. [Derivation: `core/nested-lane-projection.ts:46–97,259–275`; `verifier:659–687`; `reproduction-output.json`.]
* **Demand and terminal clearance:** preserve distinct pitch-6 lanes and terminal pins, positive stems, full terminal fans and node-body clearance. For this scene the bottom street's top edge y=712 engulfs source pins at y=716; the left street's right edge x=724 engulfs target pins at x=712. A geometry-first repair must separate these boundaries before it can reuse a perimeter-only terminal proof. [Evidence: `reproduction-output.json` → interior terminals/region bounds; `core/nested-road-capacity.ts:10–24,31–39`; `core/nested-lane-projection.ts:180–202`; `verifier:174–245,329–343`.]
* **Topology and registry:** every mouth must retain a perpendicular parent and child street contact; construction-owned turn rectangles and their unions must cover turns, with no accidental unowned section crossing. Preserve semantic door identity separately from whichever adjacent straight lanes survive. [Evidence: `core/prototype-road-registry.ts:65–89`; `core/prototype-road-network.ts:19–35,190–203`; `core/prototype-road-junction-union.ts:56–83`; `core/nested-wire-inspection.ts:96–115`.]

## Q3 — what would make the ordering legal?

**Proposed semantic candidate rule:** within each section, choose maximum IN-degree first, maximum OUT-degree among remaining nodes last, stable node-number tie breaks; fill remaining slots deterministically. Six nodes use a 3×2 row-major grid, so first/last are top-left/bottom-right of the **direct-node grid**, not the enclosing parent's entire rectangle (children live to its right). On this data it selects index first and kernel last. For a node that wins both rankings, use the IN slot then choose the next OUT candidate; that tie policy is a proposal, not existing behavior. [Evidence/basis: `evidence-summary.json` → node degrees; `core/prototype-nested-placement.ts:52–77,129–141,169–175`.]

No degree ordering is a legality theorem. The current N1-like candidate is disproved by Q1; **none of the new corner-door combinations has a legal scale-scene witness in the supplied artifacts**. D1 plus boundary-safe embedding and terminal-safe proof regions is a reasonable build candidate, not a certified result. It is also not established that certifier changes are unavoidable: a geometry change that restores disjoint two-endpoint proof regions could reuse the current certifier. Conversely, retaining swallowed terminal geometry requires a sound extension of the proof model; adding road IDs alone is insufficient. [Evidence: `../order-trial-scale/report.md:7–27`; `verifier:312–343,490–594`; `reproduction-output.json`; proposed D1 above.]
