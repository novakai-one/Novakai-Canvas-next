## Honest limits against the broad reference bar

The frozen prototype remains a dense file graph rather than a fully authored illustrated infographic. Overview text is small, several parent frames retain substantial empty space, and idle wire labels are intentionally absent under the frozen M9a contract. The generic six-crossing design guideline is not a claim made here: the required, unchanged certified totals are templates 130/0 and scale 112/0, with zero unresolved overlaps/body/boundary violations. Existing section borders are low-contrast containment separators; their geometry/width is frozen, and the new tints/tabs carry hierarchy. No new imagery, title sizes, geometry, packing or annotation policy was introduced. These inherited limits are documented rather than treated as newly solved by paint or as permission to modify layout.

The new paint has no observed blocker at overview or detail. Further adjustments would be preferences or would require changing the frozen typography/layout contract.
